Travel Tips for Kochi Commuters

1. Plan Around Metro & Ferry Timings

Many riders combine cabs with metro or ferry travel for cross-city trips. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy spots like Vyttila Mobility Hub.

2. Watch for Monsoon & Hill-Route Traffic

The Kochi-Munnar and Kochi-Thekkady highways can get congested during monsoon season and weekends. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Festival Season

Kochi's festival season, including Onam and Christmas, sees a spike in short-distance city rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Kochi's traffic variability, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.

5. Time Houseboat Pickups in Advance

For Alleppey or Kumarakom houseboat trips, booking your cab a day ahead helps align pickup timing with houseboat check-in and check-out schedules.
