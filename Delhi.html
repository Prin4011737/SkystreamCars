Travel Tips for Delhi NCR Commuters

1. Plan Around Metro-Feeder Timings

Many riders combine cabs with the Delhi Metro for cross-city travel. Booking your cab a few minutes before your metro arrives helps avoid waiting at busy stations like Rajiv Chowk or Botanical Garden.

2. Watch for Festival and Winter Fog Delays

Traffic around Diwali, Holi, and dense winter fog on the Delhi–Gurugram Expressway can add significant time. Booking slightly earlier during these periods helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Use the Expressway for Faster Gurugram–Delhi Trips

For rides between Gurugram and South Delhi, the NH-48 Expressway is often faster than the congested inner roads, especially during office hours.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Delhi's traffic variability, booking your IGI Airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.

5. Consider the DND Flyway for Noida Trips

Rides between South Delhi and Noida are often quicker via the DND Flyway during off-peak hours, avoiding the congestion around the Akshardham interchange.

6. Weekday Mornings See the Heaviest Cyber City Traffic

If your destination is Cyber City or Golf Course Road, leaving 20-30 minutes earlier than usual on weekday mornings can help you avoid the sharpest congestion around the NH-48 approach.
