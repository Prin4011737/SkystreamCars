Travel Tips for Goa Visitors

1. Plan Around Flight & Ferry Timings

Many riders combine cabs with ferry crossings for cross-river trips. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy spots like Vasco jetty.

2. Watch for Peak Tourist-Season Traffic

The Calangute-Baga and Panjim-Margao roads can get congested during peak season and festive weekends. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Festival & Wedding Season

Goa's festival and destination-wedding season sees a spike in short-distance rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Goa's seasonal traffic variability, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.

5. Time Your Beach-Belt Trips Around Sunset

Calangute, Baga and Anjuna see a rush of visitors around sunset. Booking your return ride a little early helps you avoid the evening crowd near beach shacks.
