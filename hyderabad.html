Travel Tips for Hyderabad Commuters

1. Plan Around Metro-Feeder Timings

Many riders combine cabs with Hyderabad Metro for cross-city travel. Booking your cab a few minutes before your metro arrives helps avoid waiting at busy stations like Ameerpet or Miyapur.

2. Watch for Monsoon Waterlogging

Heavy rain between June and September can slow down low-lying stretches near Hitech City and Kukatpally. Booking slightly earlier during monsoon season helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Use the ORR for Faster Cross-City Trips

For rides between distant zones like the airport and Gachibowli, the Outer Ring Road is usually faster than city roads during office hours.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Hyderabad's traffic variability, booking your RGIA ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.
