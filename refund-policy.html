Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when you're entitled to a refund or a waived cancellation charge for rides booked through Skystream Cab Service, and how those refunds are processed. It applies to city rides, outstation trips, hourly rentals, and airport transfers booked via our app, website, or 24/7 helpline, across all 500+ cities we serve.

Cancellation Charges

Exact cancellation fees vary by city and ride category and are always displayed in the app before you confirm the cancellation, so there are no surprises.

When You're Eligible for a Refund

  • Driver no-show: If a driver does not arrive within a reasonable time after acceptance and the ride is cancelled as a result, any charge is waived or refunded.
  • Duplicate or failed payment: If you're charged more than once for the same ride, or a payment fails but is still deducted, the extra amount is refunded automatically.
  • Overcharge or fare dispute: If the fare charged doesn't match the fare shown at booking (excluding legitimate add-ons like extra stops or waiting time), the difference is refunded on review.
  • Service not delivered: If a ride is cancelled by Skystream or the driver-partner for reasons outside your control, any amount already charged is refunded in full.
  • Vehicle breakdown: If your trip cannot be completed due to a vehicle breakdown and no replacement is provided, the unused portion of the fare is refunded.

When Refunds Do Not Apply

  • Cancellations made after the free-cancellation window, where the disclosed cancellation fee applies
  • Rides completed as booked, where the fare matches what was shown at booking and confirmed at the end of the trip
  • No-shows by the rider at the pickup location within the driver's waiting period
  • Charges for additional stops, waiting time, or tolls that were disclosed in the app during or after the ride

Refund Timelines & Method

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method:

  • UPI / Wallets: typically within 1-3 business days
  • Debit / Credit Cards: typically within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank
  • Cash payments: refunded as Skystream ride credit, or via bank transfer on request

Outstation & Rental Bookings

Outstation trips and multi-hour rentals may involve advance booking and driver allocation ahead of time. Cancellations made closer to the scheduled pickup time may attract a higher charge, reflecting driver allocation already made for your trip; this is always shown before you confirm a cancellation.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Open the ride in your booking history within the Skystream app.
  2. Tap "Report an Issue" and select the reason relevant to your refund request.
  3. Alternatively, contact our support team directly with your ride ID.

Refund FAQs

How long does a Skystream refund take?

UPI and wallet refunds typically arrive within 1-3 business days. Card refunds typically take 5-7 business days depending on your bank. Cash payments are refunded as ride credit or via bank transfer on request.

Is there a free cancellation window?

Yes — cancellations made within 5 minutes of booking are free. A nominal cancellation fee, shown in the app, may apply after that window.

Do I get a refund if the driver doesn't show up?

Yes. If a driver does not arrive within a reasonable time and the ride is cancelled as a result, any charge is waived or refunded in full.

What if I was charged twice for the same ride?

Duplicate payments are automatically detected and refunded to your original payment method. You can also raise it directly with our support team.
