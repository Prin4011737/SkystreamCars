Travel Tips for Indore Commuters

1. Plan Around Railway Station Timings

Many riders combine cabs with train travel for cross-city and outstation trips. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy spots like Indore Railway Station.

2. Watch for Navratri & Festive Traffic

Indore's Navratri season and festive weekends see heavy traffic across Rajwada and Sarafa. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Wedding Season

Indore's wedding season sees a spike in short-distance city rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Indore's traffic variability, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.

5. Avoid Peak Hours on AB Road

AB Road sees heavy commercial traffic on weekday evenings near the flyovers. If your destination is nearby, leaving slightly earlier can help you dodge the sharpest congestion.

6. Plan Ahead for Ujjain Temple Visits

Mahakaleshwar temple sees a spike in visitor traffic on Mondays and during major festivals — booking your Indore–Ujjain cab a little early helps you avoid the rush near the temple approach roads.
