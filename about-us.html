Our Story — From 10 Cars to 45,000+

Skystream Cab Service was founded in 2012 in Pune by a group of alumni who saw a clear gap in India's transportation ecosystem: unreliable local cabs, opaque fares, and few real safety guarantees for passengers, especially women travelling alone or at night. What began as a 10-car operation quickly grew into a technology-driven cab network built around one core idea — a rider anywhere in India deserves the same standard of safety and service.

Along the way, Skystream was among the earliest Indian cab platforms to introduce real-time GPS ride tracking, cashless digital payments, and a women-focused ride option in select cities. As the network expanded into hundreds of new towns, we invested in a dedicated Driver Academy, built an in-house dispatch and fare-prediction engine, and steadily added electric and hybrid vehicles to the fleet as part of our long-term sustainability commitment.

🏆 Key Milestones: 2012 — Founded in Pune  |  2015 — Crossed 1,000 vehicles  |  2018 — Expanded to 200+ cities  |  2020 — Launched electric vehicle fleet  |  2023 — Crossed 45,000+ vehicles  |  Today — Active across 500+ cities and every state and union territory in India.

What Sets Skystream Apart

Riders searching for the best cab service in India usually care about three things: will the driver actually show up, is the price fixed, and is the ride safe. Skystream is built around all three:

  • Verified, trained drivers: Every driver on the platform is police-verified, Aadhaar-authenticated, and trained at the Skystream Driver Academy before going live.
  • Zero-surge, upfront pricing: The fare you see before confirming a booking is the fare you pay — no festival surcharges, no last-minute price hikes.
  • Nationwide reach: With coverage in 500+ cities, Skystream offers the same standard of service whether you're booking in a metro or a small town.
  • Real safety features: Live GPS tracking, an in-app SOS button, trip-sharing with family, and passenger insurance on every ride.
  • A growing green fleet: 8,000+ electric and hybrid vehicles, with a target of 50% electric by 2027.
  • Driver-partner welfare: Transparent commissions, accidental insurance, and daily settlements for our 45,000+ driver-partners.
