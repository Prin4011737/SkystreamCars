Travel Tips for Surat Visitors

1. Plan Around Flight Timings

Many riders combine cabs with tight connecting flights out of Surat International Airport. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy pickup points.

2. Watch for Diamond & Textile Market-Hour Traffic

The Ring Road-Varachha and Katargam stretches can get congested during peak trading hours and festive weekends. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Festival & Wedding Season

Surat's festival and wedding season, especially around Navratri, sees a spike in short-distance rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Surat's growing traffic, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.
