Travel Tips for Coimbatore Commuters

1. Plan Around Railway Station Timings

Many riders combine cabs with train travel for cross-city and outstation trips. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy spots like Coimbatore Junction Railway Station.

2. Watch for Ooty-Bound Weekend Traffic

Coimbatore's weekend and holiday-season traffic towards Mettupalayam Road can build up quickly. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Wedding Season

Coimbatore's wedding season sees a spike in short-distance city rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Coimbatore's traffic variability, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.
