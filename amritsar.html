Travel Tips for Amritsar Commuters

1. Plan Around Railway Station Timings

Many riders combine cabs with train travel for cross-city and outstation trips. Booking your cab a few minutes before arrival helps avoid waiting at busy spots like Amritsar Railway Station.

2. Watch for Golden Temple Weekend Traffic

Amritsar's weekend and festival-season traffic around the Golden Temple and Hall Bazaar can build up quickly. Booking slightly earlier helps you avoid last-minute delays.

3. Book Early During Wedding Season

Amritsar's wedding season sees a spike in short-distance city rides. Booking a little ahead of your plans helps you avoid extended wait times near busy venues.

4. Book Airport Rides with Buffer Time

Given Amritsar's traffic variability, booking your airport ride at least 3 hours before a domestic flight and 4 hours before an international one is recommended.

5. Time Your Wagah Border Trip

The Beating Retreat Ceremony draws large crowds; booking a cab that accounts for security checkpoint queues near the border helps you get a good viewing spot.

6. Consider a Weekly Pass for Daily Commutes

Regular commuters between Ranjit Avenue, Lawrence Road, and business hubs on Mall Road often save with a Weekly Pass instead of booking each ride individually.
